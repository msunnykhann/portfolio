This Privacy Policy and Terms of Use apply to all mobile and web applications, software, and developer tools developed and published by Muhammad Sunny (CodeCraft by Sun) across all platforms and app stores, including productivity, document scanners, developer tools, database utilities, network tools, and security auditing applications.

2. Document & Scanner Data (100% On-Device Processing)

For document scanning, PDF utility, and image processing applications (such as NovoScanner Studio), user privacy and confidential document security are our highest priorities:

  • 100% Local Processing: All document scanning, perspective correction, image cropping, optical character recognition (OCR) text extraction, dual-side ID card generation, PDF creation, digital signing, and official stamping take place entirely on your local device.
  • Zero Cloud Uploads: We do not collect, transmit, store, or view your documents, images, signatures, stamps, or extracted text on any external servers or cloud services.
  • Data Ownership: You retain complete ownership and control over your files. All generated documents remain stored securely within your device's private application sandbox until you choose to export, share, or delete them.

To support ongoing development and provide free access to our applications, certain apps display third-party advertisements served by Google AdMob (a service provided by Google LLC).

Google AdMob may collect and process non-personally identifiable technical information in accordance with its privacy practices, including:

  • Advertising ID (Google Play AD_ID / Apple IDFA): Used to deliver ads, prevent fraud, enforce frequency capping, and show relevant ads based on device settings.
  • Device & Telemetry Data: Device model, operating system version, network connection status, and coarse location derived from IP address.

You can manage, reset, or opt out of personalized advertising at any time through your device settings (Android: Settings > Google > Ads; iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking).

4. Device Permissions

Our applications request device permissions strictly necessary for their core features:

  • Internet & Network State: Used strictly for loading advertisements through Google AdMob or executing user-initiated network requests in testing tools. Document processing and scanning operate 100% offline.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID): Used by Google AdMob to deliver compliant ads in accordance with Google Play policies.
